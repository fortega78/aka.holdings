Apartment with Antiques, Samborondón.

Samborondón, Ecuador · 2018 · Our own home · Completed

The opposite of the beach: a darker, layered home where family antiques — a carved sideboard, a bracket clock, crystal candelabra, high-backed chairs — share the room with modern pieces. Draped and tufted sofas face each other across a chevron hide rug, around a table of brass and glass.

High ceilings over the river, a dining table for twelve under a large black-and-blue work and a crystal chandelier, a gilded baroque mirror, a bar painted black around a portrait, and a study with blue brushstrokes run across walls and ceiling like a woven check. Every room is anchored by its rug: chevron hide in the living room, a printed hide under a stacked black lacquer console, a flag woven in faded reds by the bar, and black with pale flowers under a lacquered dining table.
