Over the river, Samborondón

Our own home. 2018.

The opposite of the beach: a layered home over the river where family antiques — a carved sideboard, a bracket clock, crystal candelabra — live with modern pieces. Draped, tufted sofas face each other across a chevron hide, around a table of brass and glass.

A dining room for twelve under a large black-and-blue work, a bar painted black around a portrait, and a study brushed in blue across walls and ceiling like a woven check. Every room is anchored by its rug.
