The AI diagnostic

Before any tool: where does the business lose value?

AI doesn’t transform a company. A company that changes how it works does — and AI makes that change faster, cheaper and easier to get wrong.

The diagnostic is a fixed-scope engagement for owners and operators, in English or Spanish. It answers four questions, in this order.

  1. 1
    Where is value lost?

    The handoffs, delays and blind spots that cost money or time — measured where possible, not assumed.

  2. 2
    Who owns the problem?

    Every loss gets an owner. Without one, no system will fix it.

  3. 3
    How should the work change?

    The work is redesigned on paper first. Many fixes need no AI at all.

  4. 4
    Which tool, under which rules?

    Only then the tool — and what it may decide alone, what stays with a person, and how we will know it worked.

Questions

What owners ask first.

What if AI isn’t the answer for my business?

Then the diagnostic says so, in writing, and shows what to fix first without it. Many of the losses we find are solved by changing the work, not by adding a tool.

Do I need clean data or an AI team before we start?

No. Part of the diagnostic is finding out what data you actually have, where it lives and who owns it. We work with what is there.

Will you try to sell me software?

The diagnostic stands on its own. If it points to a system, we can build it — but the recommendation is yours to act on with us or with anyone.

How do you keep our data safe?

Rules are written before anything runs: what data an agent may see, hard separation between businesses, and people approving anything irreversible.

How long does it take, and what does it cost?

Scope, duration and fee are agreed before we start, sized to your business. The first call is where we set them.

Can we work in Spanish?

Yes — the diagnostic, the documents and the meetings, in English, Spanish or both.
