Systems
In daily use

CEREBRO

Operations, verified.

A chief-of-staff system that runs the founder's day across four businesses, two academic programs and a job search. A scheduled agent drafts the morning brief at 5:45 AM from email, WhatsApp and calendar; a 15-minute ritual verifies it; a live dashboard tracks commitments, money states and habits.

  • Scheduled agent + verification ritual
  • Source-precedence and money-state rules (verified / last known / ask)
  • Decision ledger and cross-project handoffs
  • Live dashboard published as a web artifact
In build

MAGOS

Growth, orchestrated.

A multi-agent orchestration system: the layer that turns business goals into coordinated work across specialized agents, with hard per-brand data separation and human-governed autonomy levels.

  • Memory core and workflow orchestration
  • 11 governed agent seats
  • Per-brand isolation
  • Human-in-the-loop autonomy ladder
In build

FABI

Agents that do the work.

The founder's agents — the specialists MAGOS directs: listening, strategy, creative, launch, analytics, commerce, governance, reporting and transcreation — each with defined boundaries and measurable outputs.

  • 16 capability names over 11 governed seats
  • Spanish-first operating prompts
  • Reporting surface: “Ask FABI”
  • Claims and brand-safety gates
In design · co-built with a fleet operator

MULTA

Tolls and fines, handled.

MULTA (Multi-Unit Liability & Toll Agent) is an agent for car-sharing fleets of 10+ vehicles that matches every toll, ticket and fine to the right car, trip and guest — and answers the central question: who pays each charge.

  • Discovery with an operator running ~150 cars
  • Toll and violation matching per trip
  • Liability assignment and recovery workflow
  • Built on the MAGOS / FABI stack
