Nepal · stone · 2025

Dr. Lakra, in black stone.

A monkey, a skull between two cobras, a crocodile on a lotus base: Dr. Lakra’s drawing, carved by hand in black ballistic stone by stone carvers in Nepal. We worked from the artist’s drawings and proportion guides through each stage of the carving, with revisions sent between the studio and the workshop.

India · inlay · 2025

Mother of pearl and abalone, set by hand.

In Udaipur, craftspeople cut mother of pearl and abalone into small tiles and set them one by one on a drawn grid. It is the technique behind Gabriel de la Mora’s mother-of-pearl works and the surfaces we are developing for AKA Editions.

Nepal · lokta paper

Paper made by the artists’ own hands.

Lokta paper is made in the Himalayan foothills from the inner bark of the lokta bush. We brought artists to Kathmandu to make the paper themselves and to work into it with other materials, sheet by sheet.

  • Jessica StockholderWorks on lokta paper made in Kathmandu, later shown by her galleries.
  • Vargas-Suárez UniversalWorks on lokta paper made in Kathmandu.
  • Aya TakanoTravelled with us to Kathmandu; a series of hangings later shown in Paris, Tokyo and Hong Kong.
  • Abraham Cruzvillegas · Carlos AmoralesLokta collage and works with Himalayan papers.

Nepal · hand-knotting

Knot by knot, in Himalayan wool.

Tibetan hand-knotting, at up to 150 knots per square inch, in Himalayan wool with silk and yak hair where the drawing asks for it. In June 2020 we photographed hand-knotted rugs made with our partner workshop in Nepal across Patan Durbar Square.

How a production works

The artist leads. We translate the drawing into a technique and a workshop.

  1. 1ProposalThe artist’s drawing and intention, size and use.
  2. 2Technique & workshopWe match the work to the craft and to the master craftspeople who keep it alive.
  3. 3SamplesColour, material and density approved by the artist before production.
  4. 4ProductionMade by hand in the workshop, with documentation for each piece.
  5. 5DeliveryFinishing, shipping and delivery handled end to end.
Crafts

Eight techniques passed down for generations.

Hand-knotting

Nepal. Tibetan knots in Himalayan wool, with silk and yak hair.

Suzani embroidery

Uzbekistan. Hand embroidery in silk on silk-and-cotton atlas.

Felt

Kyrgyzstan. Shyrdak and ala-kiyiz, in Tien Shan wool.

Bogolan

Mali. Cotton dyed with plants and painted with fermented mud.

Lokta paper

Nepal. Paper from the bark of the lokta bush, made by the artists.

Mother of pearl and abalone

India. Inlay set by hand, tile by tile.

Stone

Nepal. Sculpture carved by hand; jade mosaic in development in Mexico and India.

Hair

India. Works in human hair with Gabriel de la Mora.
