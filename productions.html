Miami 02 / Art Productions

What we produce.

AKA Art Productions produces work for contemporary artists together with master craftspeople in Nepal, India, Mexico, Uzbekistan and Mali, bringing intangible cultural heritage — hand-knotting, embroidery, weaving — into contemporary art.

Each production is a collaboration: the artist's drawing, the workshop's technique, and our direction from the first sample to the finished piece.

Where the work has been shown.

Work we have produced has been exhibited at Art Basel Miami Beach, Art Basel Paris, Art Week Tokyo, FIAC Paris, Zona Maco, NADA Miami and Museo Jumex.
