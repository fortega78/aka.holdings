Edgewater Residence, Miami.

Miami · 2026 · Residential · Full service

A bay-front apartment in Edgewater, designed end to end: living room, kitchen, entry and study, with custom millwork throughout. A gradient relief above the sofa sets the palette for the whole home; fluted oak, lit niches and a wine wall turn the media wall into one piece of furniture joined to the kitchen.

Coordinated with the building’s requirements and a general contractor; purchasing, deliveries and installation run by the studio.
