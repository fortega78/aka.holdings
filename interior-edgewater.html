Full service. Completed 2026.

A bay-front apartment designed end to end: living room, kitchen, entry and study. Custom millwork in fluted oak runs from the kitchen to the living room and holds the television, a wine wall and lit niches.

The gradient relief above the sofa set the palette for every room. The work was coordinated with the building and a general contractor; purchasing, deliveries and installation were run by the studio.
