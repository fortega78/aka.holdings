Project 04

Oceanfront Apartment, Ecuador.

Ecuador coast · 2018 · Our own home · Completed

An apartment over the Pacific, designed and furnished by Fabian himself, room by room. White linen sofas held in solid red-wood blocks, a marble table on a crowd of oak legs, and a wall of geometric works that spills across the plaster. One wall is painted in an unfinished stroke of blue, so the room never looks done.

Over the dining table, a cluster of bare bulbs on long black cables; the table itself is a patchwork of oak blocks under glass, facing the sea. In the bedroom, the bed is framed by four tree trunks. On the roof, a pergola of cane over a floor of patterned tiles.

Design · furniture made to order · art and objects · terrace

Design and furnishing: Fabian Ortega · Our own home, photographed as lived in.
