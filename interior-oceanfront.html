Penthouse Punta Barandúa — Ecuador

Our own home. 2018.

An apartment over the Pacific, designed and furnished by Fabian himself, room by room. White linen sofas held in solid red-wood blocks, a marble table on a crowd of oak legs, and a wall of geometric works that spills across the plaster.

Over the dining table, bare bulbs on long black cables; the table is a patchwork of oak blocks under glass, facing the sea. In the bedroom, the bed is framed by four tree trunks. On the roof, a pergola of cane over patterned tiles.
