What we do differently

Five habits — each with a project behind it, not a promise.

  1. I

    We enter through the art.

    The first question is never the sofa — it is the piece the room answers to. In Samborondón, a red op-art work chose the chair beside it.

  2. II

    Every piece carries its reason, in writing.

    You approve reasons, not moods. In the collector’s apartment, the round work set the axis of the living room — and the list says why.

  3. III

    The plan is verified before a dollar is spent.

    Every piece is placed to scale first. In Quito that meant six dining chairs instead of eight — caught on paper, not in the freight elevator.

  4. IV

    We can make the piece that doesn’t exist.

    Through AKA Art Productions, with master craftspeople in eight countries. For the Quito nook: Jessica Stockholder, lokta paper, made in Kathmandu with us.

  5. V

    One person, from the first plan to the last book.

    Measuring, proposal, purchasing, installers and styling — one interlocutor for all of it. Edgewater ran this way, end to end.

How we work

Four steps, in order. Nothing is bought before its reason is approved on paper.

  1. 01
    Consultation

    At your home or online. We measure, listen, and you leave with a written plan: what to keep, what to move, what to buy.

  2. 02
    Proposal

    A furnished plan to scale, the palette and every piece with its reason. You approve it before anything is spent.

  3. 03
    Purchasing

    Through our trade accounts with more than ten furniture, lighting and textile brands. You see the retail price next to yours.

  4. 04
    Installation

    Deliveries, installers and millwork coordinated by us; the home styled down to the last book.
