Miami 01 / Interior Design

A home begins with one piece.

We design homes and spaces in Miami starting from what the place and its owner already have: the artwork, the object, the light, the neighbourhood. Materials, furniture and colour follow from there — not the other way round.

We work with the artists, craftspeople and galleries we have produced with for years, and with our own living library of pieces, sources and references.

How we work.

First the style: a style card built from your real references. Then the scheme: furniture, paint and art, with compared options and measurements. Then the plan to scale, with circulation and clearances verified. Finally, the package to execute and purchase.

Residential and commercial projects. Proposals in English or Spanish.
