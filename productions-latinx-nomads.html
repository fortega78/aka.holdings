Latinx Nomads: filming in Tbilisi and Mtskheta.

Propose a project

In October 2021 we took the camera to Georgia to film Latinx Nomads: six Latinx influencers discovering the country’s art and culture, made to bring Georgia to a wider audience.

The series includes interviews with a former president of Georgia and with Tekuna Gachechiladze, the country’s best-known chef, with several restaurants. We filmed in Tbilisi; at Mtskheta, the old capital above the meeting of two rivers; and in the vaulted cellar of one of Georgia’s leading wineries.
