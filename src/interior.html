What we do differently

Plenty of studios make beautiful rooms. These five habits are ours — and each one has a project behind it, not a promise.

I

We enter through the art.

The first question is never the sofa — it is the piece the room will answer to. A red op-art work decides the chair that sits beside it; a Matta decides a whole living room.

{{fig:r3-ant-red-opart-chair|A red op-art work beside a woven-leather safari chair|The work decides the chair beside it.}}
II

Every piece carries its reason, in writing.

Not a mood board — a list where each piece argues for its presence: why this red, why chrome, why nothing else on the table. You approve reasons, not vibes.

{{fig:colf-bronze|The dining table with caned chairs and the gold bronze alone at its centre|From the collector’s list: “the bronze sits alone at its centre.”}}
III

The plan is verified before a dollar is spent.

We rebuild the architect’s plan in centimetres and place every piece to scale — clearances, door swings, rug sizes. In Quito that meant six dining chairs instead of eight, caught on paper, not in the freight elevator.

{{fig:qtf-plan-final|The final furnished plan of the Quito apartment, every piece numbered|Quito: the final plan, verified in centimetres.}}
IV

We can make — or produce — the piece that doesn’t exist.

Through AKA Art Productions we work with master craftspeople in eight countries. When the right work cannot be bought, it can be made: a felt triptych drawn for a dining wall, or a work born in our own productions, chosen for a quiet corner.

{{fig:r3-stockholder-lokta-work|A burgundy work in handmade lokta paper with embroidered threads, in an acrylic box|Jessica Stockholder, lokta paper, made in Kathmandu with AKA Art Productions — chosen for the Quito nook.}}
V

One person, from the first plan to the last book.

Measuring, proposal, trade purchasing, deliveries, installers, millwork and the final styling — one interlocutor for all of it. Edgewater ran this way, end to end.

How we work

Four steps, in order. Nothing is bought before its reason is approved on paper.

  1. Consultation

    At your home or online. We measure, listen, and you leave with a written plan: what to keep, what to move, what to buy and in what order.

  2. Proposal

    A furnished plan to scale, the palette and every piece with the reason it is there. You approve it before anything is spent.

  3. Purchasing

    Through our trade accounts with more than ten furniture, lighting and textile brands. You see the retail price next to yours.

  4. Installation

    Deliveries, installers and millwork coordinated by us, and the home styled down to the last book.
