Collector’s Apartment, Miami.

2026 · Collection styling · Proposal

More than twenty works in an open-plan apartment. Nothing leaves the collection and nothing is added: it is rearranged and lit, and the layer around it is rewritten — few pieces with a signature, and one thread of colour taken from the art.

A warm altar on the amber cart beneath the painting.

How we work

Four steps, one point of contact.

  1. 1ConsultationAt your home or online. We measure, listen and leave with scope and priorities: what to keep, what to move, what to buy and in what order.
  2. 2ProposalA furnished plan to scale, the palette and every piece with its reason. You approve it before spending.
  3. 3PurchasingThrough our trade accounts at more than ten furniture, lighting and textile brands: you see the retail price next to yours.
  4. 4InstallationDeliveries, installers and millwork coordinated, and the home styled down to the last book.
